# tabular-context-builder benchmark

Bounded previews backed by complete, hashed CSV evidence.

- Source: deterministic synthetic fixtures generated by GEPASE.
- License: Apache-2.0.
- Cases: 50 (30 train, 10 validation, 10 test).
- Minimum acceptance evidence: E3 executable assertions.
- Score composition: 80% deterministic assertions, 20% blind quality rubric.
- Limitation: fixtures isolate artifact correctness; they do not represent every production integration or subjective user preference.
